# FinTech Customer Transaction & Risk Analytics — Business Insights

## Executive Summary
This project analyzed synthetic FinTech transaction data to evaluate customer behavior, transaction volume, fee revenue, failed payments, payment channel performance, merchant category performance, customer segments, and risk indicators.

The analysis supports the type of reporting used by FinTech companies, digital banks, payment platforms, financial operations teams, and business analytics teams.

## Key Findings
- The platform processed a large volume of transactions across multiple customer segments, payment channels, and merchant categories.
- Fee revenue was mainly driven by completed transactions and higher-volume merchant categories.
- Failed transactions created operational concerns because they may affect customer experience and reduce expected revenue.
- Payment channel analysis showed that some channels had higher failure activity than others.
- Merchant category analysis helped identify where customers were spending the most and which categories carried higher average risk.
- Customer segment analysis showed differences in transaction volume, fee revenue, and failed payment activity.
- Risk flag analysis helped identify transactions that may require additional review by fraud, compliance, or operations teams.

## Business Recommendations
- Monitor payment channels with higher failure rates to reduce customer friction.
- Review high-risk transactions regularly, especially large transactions and failed transactions.
- Track failed transaction reasons to identify repeated operational or payment processing issues.
- Prioritize merchant categories with high volume and high risk for closer monitoring.
- Use dashboards to help operations and business teams monitor performance quickly.
- Continue tracking customer segment performance to support product, marketing, and risk decisions.
